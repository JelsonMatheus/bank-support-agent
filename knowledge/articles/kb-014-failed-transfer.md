If a transfer you sent didn't go through, one of these is usually the reason.

## Why a transfer can fail

- **The IBAN is invalid.** Check that the recipient's IBAN is complete and
  correct, with no missing or swapped characters.
- **The recipient's bank rejected it.** For example, the recipient's account
  may be closed. You may want to check the account details with the
  recipient.
- **Your balance was insufficient.** Make sure there's enough money in your
  account to cover the transfer.

## What happens to your money

If a transfer fails, the money is returned to your Orbis Bank account within
2 working days.

## Before you try again

Once you've found the reason, you can send the transfer again with the
correct details. Transfers are free, so sending it again won't cost you
anything.
