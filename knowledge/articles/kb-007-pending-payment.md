## Why a payment shows as pending

When you pay by card, the payment shows as Pending until the merchant confirms
it. This is normal and usually happens within 1–3 working days.

## Payments that take longer

Hotels, car rentals and fuel stations can take up to 7 days to confirm a
payment. They may also place a temporary hold that is larger than the amount
you finally pay.

## If the final amount is different

The final amount can differ from the pending amount you first see.

## If the merchant doesn't confirm the payment

If the merchant doesn't confirm a pending amount within 7 days, it's released
automatically.

## What we can and can't do

Orbis Bank can't speed up or cancel a pending payment, because it depends on
the merchant confirming it.

If you think a charge is incorrect, please contact support through the in-app
chat or by email at support@orbisbank.example.
