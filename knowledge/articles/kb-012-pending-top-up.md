How long a top-up takes depends on how you added the money. If you're waiting
for a top-up, check which method you used and compare it with the times below.

## How long each method takes

| Method | When it arrives |
| --- | --- |
| Debit card top-up in the app | Usually instantly, but it can take up to 30 minutes |
| SEPA Instant bank transfer | In seconds |
| Standard SEPA bank transfer | Within 1 working day |

## When to contact support

If a bank transfer top-up hasn't arrived after 2 working days, please contact
support through the in-app chat or by email at support@orbisbank.example, and
include proof of the transfer.
