If a card top-up didn't go through, one of these is usually the reason.

## Why a card top-up can fail

- **Your card issuer declined it.** The bank that issued your card didn't
  approve the payment.
- **The 3-D Secure check failed.** The security check for the payment wasn't
  completed successfully.
- **The card isn't in your name.** You can only top up with a card in your own
  name.
- **It's a credit card.** Credit cards are not accepted for top-ups. Please
  use a debit card.
- **You've reached the monthly maximum.** Card top-ups are limited to €10,000
  per calendar month.

## What happens to your money

A failed card top-up is not charged.

If your other bank shows a pending charge for the failed top-up, that bank
will release it, usually within 5 working days.

## Other ways to add money

You can also add money with a bank transfer to your Orbis IBAN. Bank transfer
top-ups are free.
