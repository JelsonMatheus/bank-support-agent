category: account
---

You can keep your details up to date in the app. How you change them depends
on which detail it is.

## Email and phone number

1. Go to Settings → Personal details.
2. Enter your new email or phone number.
3. Enter the confirmation code we send to your new email or phone.

## Name and address

Name and address changes are requested in the app and need a supporting
document:

- **Name change:** an ID document.
- **Address change:** a proof of address dated within the last 3 months.

We review requests within 2 working days.

## Date of birth

Your date of birth can't be changed in the app. If it's wrong, please contact
support through the in-app chat or by email at support@orbisbank.example.
