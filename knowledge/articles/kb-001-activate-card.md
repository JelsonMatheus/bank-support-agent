Your Orbis Bank debit card needs to be activated in the app before you can use
it for the first time. It only takes a moment.

## How to activate your card

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Activate card.
3. Enter the last 4 digits of the card number printed on your card.

Once that's done, your card is ready to use.

## When contactless starts working

Contactless payments won't work straight away. They start working after you
make your first payment with chip and PIN, so for that first purchase, insert
your card into the terminal and enter your PIN.

If you need to check your PIN before paying, you can see it in the app at
Cards → Show PIN. You'll be asked for your passcode first.

After your first chip and PIN payment, you can tap to pay wherever contactless
is accepted.
