You open the Orbis Bank app with a 6-digit passcode. If you've forgotten it,
you can reset it yourself in a few steps. You'll need access to both your
email and your phone.

## How to reset your passcode

1. On the app's login screen, tap "Forgot passcode".
2. Open the link we send to your email.
3. Confirm with the code we send to your phone by SMS.
4. Choose your new 6-digit passcode.

You can then open the app with your new passcode.

## If you can't access your email or phone

If you no longer have access to your email or your phone, you won't be able to
complete these steps yourself. Please contact support, and we'll verify your
identity before helping you regain access. You can email us at
support@orbisbank.example, and we'll reply within 1 working day.
