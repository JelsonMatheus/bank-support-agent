category: transfers

Orbis Bank supports SEPA transfers in euros only, and transfers are free.
How long a transfer takes depends on whether it's a SEPA Instant or a standard
SEPA transfer.

## SEPA Instant transfers

- Arrive within seconds, 24/7, including weekends and public holidays.
- Only work if the recipient's bank supports SEPA Instant.
- Can be up to €100,000 per transfer.

## Standard SEPA transfers

Standard SEPA transfers depend on the 15:00 cut-off time (Lisbon time):

- **Sent before 15:00 on a working day:** the money arrives on the next
  working day.
- **Sent after 15:00, on a weekend or on a public holiday:** the transfer is
  processed on the next working day.

For example, a standard SEPA transfer sent on a Tuesday at 10:00 arrives on
Wednesday, as long as Wednesday is a working day.
