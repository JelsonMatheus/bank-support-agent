## Your daily spending limit

Your card has a daily spending limit of €2,500 by default. You can set it to
any amount between €0 and €5,000 in the app.

## How to change your daily limit

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Limits.
3. Choose a new daily limit between €0 and €5,000.

If you reach your daily limit, your card payments can be declined.

## Switching online payments on or off

You can choose whether your card can be used for online payments.

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Security.
3. Switch online payments on or off.

If online payments are switched off, any online payment you try to make with
your card will be declined.
