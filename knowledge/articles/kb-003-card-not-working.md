If your card isn't working, these checks usually find the cause. Go through
them in order in the app.

## Checks to try

1. **Is your card activated?** A new card must be activated before its first
   use. Go to Cards → Activate card and enter the last 4 digits of the card
   number.
2. **Is your card frozen?** A frozen card declines all payments. Go to
   Cards → Freeze card to unfreeze it.
3. **Is your PIN blocked?** Your PIN is blocked after 3 incorrect attempts. Go
   to Cards → Unblock PIN. Your PIN stays the same.
4. **Have you reached your daily limit?** Check your daily spending limit in
   Cards → Limits.
5. **Paying online?** Make sure online payments are switched on in
   Cards → Security.
6. **Do you have enough money?** Check your balance before trying again.

## Replacing a damaged or expired card

If your card is damaged or expired, you can replace it in the app at
Cards → Replace card.

A replacement for a damaged card costs €5. Cards that are about to expire are
replaced automatically and for free, so you don't need to do anything.

Replacement cards arrive in the same time as new cards: 5–7 working days after
dispatch.
