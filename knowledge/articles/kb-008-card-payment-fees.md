## When you pay a fee

- **Card payments in euros** are free.
- **Card payments in other currencies** have a fee of 1% of the payment
  amount.

## Merchant surcharges

Some merchants add their own surcharge when you pay by card. This surcharge is
set by the merchant, not by Orbis Bank, so if you have questions about it,
please ask the merchant.

## Where to see a fee

Any fee is shown in the transaction details in the app. Open the payment in
your transaction list to see it.

## If you think a fee is wrong

If you think you've been charged a fee incorrectly, please contact support
through the in-app chat or by email at support@orbisbank.example.
