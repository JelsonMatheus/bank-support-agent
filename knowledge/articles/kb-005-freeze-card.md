title: Freezing and unfreezing your card
category: cards
---

You can freeze your card in the app at any time, and unfreeze it whenever
you're ready. Both take effect immediately.

## How to freeze your card

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Freeze card.

## How to unfreeze your card

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Freeze card again to unfreeze it.

Your card works again as soon as it's unfrozen.

## What happens while your card is frozen

A frozen card declines all payments. If a payment is declined and you're not
sure why, check whether your card is frozen.

## If your card is lost, stolen or compromised

Freeze your card in the app straight away, then contact us through the in-app
chat. We'll treat it as urgent.
