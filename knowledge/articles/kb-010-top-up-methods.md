There are two ways to add money to your Orbis Bank account.

## 1. Bank transfer to your Orbis IBAN

Send a bank transfer from another account to your Orbis IBAN.

- **Fee:** bank transfer top-ups are free.
- **Timing:** SEPA Instant transfers arrive in seconds. Standard SEPA
  transfers arrive within 1 working day.

## 2. Debit card top-up in the app

Add money in the app using a debit card.

- **Fee:** free up to €3,000 per calendar month. Above that, there's a fee of
  0.5%.
- **Limit:** you can top up a maximum of €10,000 per calendar month by card.
- **Timing:** card top-ups usually arrive instantly, but can take up to 30
  minutes.

## Which cards you can use

- The card must be a debit card. Credit cards are not accepted for top-ups.
- The card must be in your own name.
