Your debit card is ordered automatically when you open your account. We send
it once your identity verification is complete.

## How long delivery takes

Your card is sent by standard post and arrives 5–7 working days after it's
dispatched. Because it's sent by standard post, there is no tracking number.

## Checking your card status in the app

The app shows where your card is in the process:

- **Ordered**: your card has been ordered but hasn't been sent yet.
- **Dispatched**: your card is on its way. The app also shows the dispatch
  date, so you can work out when to expect it.

## If your card hasn't arrived

If your card still hasn't arrived 10 working days after the dispatch date,
please contact support through the in-app chat or by email at
support@orbisbank.example. Your first replacement card is free.
