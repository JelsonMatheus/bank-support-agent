A declined card payment can be frustrating. Here are the most common reasons
and what you can check in the app.

## Common reasons and what to check

1. **Not enough balance.** Check that your balance covers the payment.
2. **Your card is frozen.** A frozen card declines all payments. Go to
   Cards → Freeze card to unfreeze it.
3. **Your card isn't activated.** Go to Cards → Activate card and enter the
   last 4 digits of the card number.
4. **Your PIN is blocked.** Go to Cards → Unblock PIN.
5. **You've reached your daily limit.** Check or adjust it in Cards → Limits.
6. **Online payments are switched off.** Switch them on in Cards → Security.

## When the merchant declines the payment

Sometimes the merchant declines the payment on their side. When that happens,
Orbis Bank can't see the merchant's reason, so it's best to ask the merchant
directly.

## You won't be charged

Declined payments are not charged. If a declined payment shows as pending in
your app, the amount is released within 7 days.
