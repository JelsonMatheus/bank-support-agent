Our support team is here to help. You can reach us in two ways.

## In-app chat

Chat with us in the app every day, from 08:00 to 22:00 (Lisbon time).

## Email

Write to us at support@orbisbank.example. We reply within 1 working day.

## If your card is lost, stolen or compromised

If your card is lost or stolen, or you think someone else may have your card
details, act straight away:

1. Freeze your card in the app: go to Cards → Freeze card.
2. Contact us through the in-app chat and tell us what happened.

We handle these reports as urgent.

## If you think a charge is wrong

If you see a charge on your account that you think is incorrect, please
contact support through the in-app chat or by email.
