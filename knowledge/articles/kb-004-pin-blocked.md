## Why your PIN is blocked

For your security, your PIN is blocked after 3 incorrect attempts. While it's
blocked, your card payments can be declined.

## How to unblock your PIN

You can unblock your PIN yourself in the app:

1. Open the Orbis Bank app.
2. Go to Cards → Unblock PIN.

Your PIN is unblocked immediately, and it stays the same, so you can keep
using the PIN you already have.

## How to see your PIN

If you're not sure what your PIN is, check it in the app before trying again,
so you don't block it a second time:

1. Go to Cards → Show PIN.
2. Enter your passcode.

Your PIN will be shown on screen.
